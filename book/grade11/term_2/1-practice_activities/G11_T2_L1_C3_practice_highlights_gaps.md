## Definition specialization without new records

The ecosystem does support sample size, population SD, population mean, sample mean, SE, confidence, critical value, margin and full width. The expanded reference uses context definitions for sample size and population SD, the population-mean meaning, and effective brief fields for confidence, critical value and width where suitable.

Some available definitions cover a broader course scope than this known-population-SD normal lesson. In particular, `g11-t2-c3-coordinated-11-se` includes estimated SE and proportion cases; `...-critical` includes Student cutoffs and target confidence; `...-meansample` includes an indexed mean of another sample. The local reference uses the existing introductory formulas and specific sample-mean interpretation instead of importing those extra cases. This is local specialization, not missing mathematical support. No indexed notation was shortened or reassigned.

The local reference preserves individual-observation variability versus sampling variability, estimate versus fixed parameter, nominal confidence versus cutoff, and centre-to-bound margin versus full width. The original Introduction retains its sampling, independence, known-SD and normal-approximation assumptions and its rounding policy. Questions, solutions, labels, navigation commands, and toggles are outside the table changes; no Olympiad material is involved and no duplicate practice source was created.
