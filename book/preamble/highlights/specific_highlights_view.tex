\maketitle
This English reference collects the live symbols and concepts for Grades 10
and 11. Term 1 and Term 2 data are available; Term 3 references are pending.
The opening tables give brief meanings. The detailed references distinguish
mathematical definitions from introductory-example interpretations where those
interpretations are registered. Repeated full tables are intentional.

Semantic colours reuse the shared roles and local palette settings. Names,
notation and units distinguish meanings, including different uses of the same
symbol. Prerequisite notation retains its source grade and term; it does not
supply a Term 3 reference. Highlights are enabled.
