Introduction to Analysis of Decisions.

  • Characterise decisions under uncertainty: identify the decision alternatives, states of nature, consequences, and economic or financial context.
  • Organise and calculate payoffs: structure alternatives, states of nature, and payoffs in payoff tables; when needed, calculate profit from revenues and fixed and variable costs.
  • Apply criteria without probabilities: use Maximax for the highest possible payoff, Maximin for the best worst-case payoff, and Minimax Regret to minimise the maximum opportunity loss using an opportunity loss table.
  • Apply criteria with probabilities: calculate Expected Monetary Value from stated or estimated probabilities and use the Bayes decision criterion to select the highest expected payoff.
  • Test probability assumptions: use subjective probabilities and sensitivity analysis to determine how probability changes affect the preferred decision.
  • Compare decision criteria: contrast Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes by their information requirements, risk orientations, recommended alternatives, and limitations.
  • Build and solve decision trees: distinguish decision nodes from chance nodes, assign probabilities and payoffs, and use backward induction to identify an optimal strategy.
  • Evaluate and justify decisions: select the most appropriate criterion for an economic or financial scenario based on the available information, risk attitude, and decision context.
