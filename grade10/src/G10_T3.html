C1

Characterises continuous random variables by interpreting probability density functions, recognising that probabilities correspond to areas under curves and that \(P(X=x)=0\).

C2

Applies the uniform continuous distribution on an interval \([a,b]\) to calculate probabilities as areas of rectangles in economic and financial contexts.

C3

Applies probability rules for simple, combined, and complementary events across discrete and continuous probability models to calculate and interpret likelihoods in economic and financial contexts.

C4

Uses frequency distributions, quartiles, percentiles, and graphical representations to describe empirical economic data and compare its shape, centre, and spread with a continuous probability model.

C5

Applies the standard normal distribution \(Z \sim N(0,1)\) and standard normal tables to calculate probabilities, critical values, and probability areas in finance and economics contexts.

C6

Uses standardisation \(Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma\) to transform normal random variables and solve probability problems involving real-life economic and financial data.

C7

Applies inverse normal reasoning to find values from given probabilities, interpreting percentiles, confidence-related values, and value at risk in financial contexts.

C8

Interprets the mean and variance of continuous probability distributions through graphical and contextual representations.

C9

Analyses investment portfolios by interpreting assets, weights, expected returns, variance, covariance, and correlation as elements that determine portfolio risk.

C10

Evaluates financial-risk situations using continuous distributions, including normal, to interpret uncertainty, portfolio behaviour and preparation for statistical estimation.

  • PDF (f(x)): Probability density; total area under curve is 1.
  • CDF (F(x)): Probability X ≤ x, found by integrating the PDF.
  • Expected value: E[X] = ∫ x·f(x) dx; Variance: Var(X) = ∫ (x−μ)^2 f(x) dx.
  • Normal(μ, σ²): Bell-shaped; standardize with Z = (X−μ)/σ for table lookup.
  • Exponential(λ): Time between Poisson events; PDF λe^{−λx} for x ≥ 0; memoryless.
  • Uniform(a, b): Constant density 1/(b−a) on [a, b]; mean (a+b)/2.
