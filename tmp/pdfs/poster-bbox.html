Its allowed values and meaning depend on the context. Possible values in separate cases Each choice below is a separate case. Ordinary numbers: x can be −2, 0, or 3.5. Integers: n can be 1, 2, 3, . . . when counting objects. Angles: θ can be 30 ◦ , 60 ◦ , or 90 ◦ . Sets: A can be {1, 2} or {0, 3, 5}. Greek letters as numbers: λ can be −1, 0, or 2. Letters do not have permanently assigned types. Uppercase letters often represent sets, and Greek letters often represent angles or numbers, but the context determines their meaning. Symbols acquire meaning from their mathematical context.
