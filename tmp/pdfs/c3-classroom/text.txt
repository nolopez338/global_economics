of error, explaining how each component affects precision and reliability.

At fixed SE, z ∗ = MoE/3 is a line through the origin. Higher confidence requires a larger cutoff and margin, while SE stays
unchanged. The smooth confidence curves use 100[2Φ(z ∗ ) − 1], or 100[2Φ(MoE/3) − 1], where Φ is the standard normal
CDF. The five points use the nominal supplied percentages. Rounding the cutoffs causes a very small difference between
these points and the theoretical curves.
