Learning objective
Model discrete monetary outcomes in economic and financial contexts; derive and verify probability mass functions (PMFs), and
construct and interpret cumulative distribution functions (CDFs) with tables and graphs.


Criteria assessment
This standalone key authors the original questions required by the governing assessment brief and reproduces each task before its
worked solution. Questions 1–2 assess C1; Questions 3–6 assess C2. Show reasoning and exact probabilities. All contextual rules are
stated in ordinary language.
Independent passing thresholds: C1 requires at least 4 out of 8 points; C2 requires at least 4 out of 14 points.
Points cannot transfer between criteria. The overall available total is 22 points. An overall total alone does not establish a
pass in both criteria. These are the passing thresholds for this assessment; no cumulative policy is specified.


Evaluated criteria
C1 Characterises discrete random variables in economic and financial contexts by identifying their support, probability mass function
    and possible outcomes.
C2 Interprets PMFs and CDFs by verifying their defining properties and using them to calculate point, interval, cumulative, and
     complementary probabilities in financial contexts.

1    Online order contribution                                                                                                 [C1-4]
A small seller handles three orders, in first, second and third order. Each order independently has exactly two mutually exclusive
possible outcomes: the buyer keeps it with probability 0.6, or cancels it with probability 0.4. These probabilities apply to every order.
Each kept order contributes 5 USD and each cancellation contributes no money. A fixed handling cost of 4 USD is then deducted
once from the combined contribution. There are no other receipts or costs. Record the net contribution for the three orders.
Original Task 1 (proposed allocation). List every complete outcome in order and calculate its probability.                           [1]

Worked solution to Task 1                                                                                                            [1]
Write K for kept and C for cancelled. There are 23 = 8 ordered complete outcomes. Independence permits multiplication within
each triple; it does not make the triples equally likely.

                                         Ordered outcome       Product          Exact probability
                                               KKK           0.6 · 0.6 · 0.6      27/125 = 0.216
                                               KKC           0.6 · 0.6 · 0.4      18/125 = 0.144
                                               KCK           0.6 · 0.4 · 0.6      18/125 = 0.144
                                               KCC           0.6 · 0.4 · 0.4      12/125 = 0.096
                                               CKK           0.4 · 0.6 · 0.6      18/125 = 0.144
                                               CKC           0.4 · 0.6 · 0.4      12/125 = 0.096
                                               CCK           0.4 · 0.4 · 0.6      12/125 = 0.096
                                               CCC           0.4 · 0.4 · 0.4      8/125 = 0.064

The eight probabilities sum to (3/5 + 2/5)3 = 1.
Proposed partial credit. 0.5 for all eight ordered outcomes; 0.5 for their probabilities using independent products. Total: 1 point.


Original Task 2 (proposed allocation). Define the random variable and its units. Map every complete outcome to a value,
identify the support, and construct and verify the grouped PMF.                                                    [2]

Worked solution to Task 2                                                                                                            [2]
Let X be the net contribution from the three orders, in USD. If N is the number kept, X = 5N − 4. The table maps all eight
outcomes and groups those with the same net contribution.

                               x (USD)     Complete outcomes                   Grouped probability
                                  −4             CCC                         8/125 = 8/125
                                   1        KCC, CKC, CCK          12/125 + 12/125 + 12/125 = 36/125
                                   6        KKC, KCK, CKK          18/125 + 18/125 + 18/125 = 54/125
                                  11             KKK                        27/125 = 27/125

Thus SX = {−4, 1, 6, 11} USD. The PMF is the last column, and is zero elsewhere. All masses are positive, and (8+36+54+27)/125 = 1.
A negative value means the handling cost exceeds receipts.
Proposed partial credit. 0.25 for the variable and units; 0.5 for all outcome mappings; 0.25 for support; 0.75 for grouped PMF
masses; 0.25 for positivity and normalization checks. Total: 2 points.


Original Task 3 (proposed allocation). Calculate and interpret the probability that the net contribution is at least 6 USD. [1]

Worked solution to Task 3                                                                                                            [1]
From the grouped PMF, P (X ≥ 6) = pX (6) + pX (11) = 54/125 + 27/125 = 81/125 = 0.648. The seller earns a net contribution of at
least 6 USD with probability 64.8%; this happens exactly when at least two orders are kept.
Proposed partial credit. 0.5 for selecting and adding the correct masses; 0.5 for the contextual interpretation. Total: 1 point.
Question total: 4 points for C1.

2    Two supplier loyalty credits                                                                                          [C1-4]
A retailer receives one loyalty credit from each of two suppliers. The suppliers award credits independently. Each has exactly three
mutually exclusive possible outcomes: no credit, a 3 USD credit, or an 8 USD credit. For the first supplier these probabilities are
0.2, 0.5 and 0.3, respectively. For the second supplier they are 0.4, 0.4 and 0.2, respectively. Add both credits; there are no other
adjustments. Record the total credit, keeping first-supplier then second-supplier order.
Original Task 1 (proposed allocation). List every complete ordered outcome and calculate its probability.                         [1]

Worked solution to Task 1                                                                                                        [1]
Each supplier has three possible outcomes, giving 32 = 9 ordered pairs. Write a pair as its two credit amounts, in USD. Multiply the
appropriate probabilities by independence.

                                  Ordered credit outcome (USD)            Product     Exact probability
                                                (0, 0)                    0.2 · 0.4     2/25 = 0.08
                                                (0, 3)                    0.2 · 0.4     2/25 = 0.08
                                                (0, 8)                    0.2 · 0.2     1/25 = 0.04
                                                (3, 0)                    0.5 · 0.4      1/5 = 0.20
                                                (3, 3)                    0.5 · 0.4      1/5 = 0.20
                                                (3, 8)                    0.5 · 0.2     1/10 = 0.10
                                                (8, 0)                    0.3 · 0.4     3/25 = 0.12
                                                (8, 3)                    0.3 · 0.4     3/25 = 0.12
                                                (8, 8)                    0.3 · 0.2     3/50 = 0.06

Exhaustiveness check: (0.2 + 0.5 + 0.3)(0.4 + 0.4 + 0.2) = 1.
Proposed partial credit. 0.5 for all nine ordered pairs; 0.5 for independent product probabilities. Total: 1 point.


Original Task 2 (proposed allocation). Define the random variable and units; map every outcome to a value; identify the
support; construct and verify the grouped PMF.                                                                      [2]

Worked solution to Task 2                                                                                                        [2]
Let X be the total loyalty credit from the two suppliers, in USD. Add the two amounts in each pair. The complete mapping and
grouped PMF are:

                                       x (USD)     Ordered outcomes          Grouped probability
                                           0                 (0, 0)               2/25 = 2/25
                                           3             (0, 3), (3, 0)        2/25 + 1/5 = 7/25
                                           6                 (3, 3)                1/5 = 1/5
                                           8             (0, 8), (8, 0)       1/25 + 3/25 = 4/25
                                          11             (3, 8), (8, 3)      1/10 + 3/25 = 11/50
                                          16                 (8, 8)               3/50 = 3/50

The support is SX = {0, 3, 6, 8, 11, 16} USD, with zero mass elsewhere. All masses are positive and (4 + 14 + 10 + 8 + 11 + 3)/50 =
50/50 = 1. Reversing a pair can preserve its monetary value while changing its probability.
Proposed partial credit. 0.25 for variable and units; 0.5 for all mappings; 0.25 for support; 0.75 for grouped masses; 0.25 for
positivity and normalization checks. Total: 2 points.


Original Task 3 (proposed allocation). Calculate and interpret the probability that the total credit is at least 11 USD.         [1]

Worked solution to Task 3                                                                                                        [1]
P (X ≥ 11) = pX (11) + pX (16) = 11/50 + 3/50 = 7/25 = 0.28. The retailer receives a total credit of at least 11 USD with probability
28%; the qualifying pairs are (3, 8), (8, 3), (8, 8).
Proposed partial credit. 0.5 for selecting and adding the correct masses; 0.5 for the contextual interpretation. Total: 1 point.
Question total: 4 points for C1. Criterion C1 total: 8 points; independent pass: at least 4 points.

5    Marketplace account credits                                                                                               [C2-4]

possible credits and their categories are mutually exclusive. A 2 USD credit is twice as likely as no credit. A 5 USD credit is as

likely as a 9 USD credit. A 20 USD credit is as likely as the 2 USD and 14 USD credits combined. There are no other payments or
adjustments. Determine the probabilities from these likelihood relationships and record the account credit.

6     Community market prize tickets                                                                                                  [C2-6]
Advanced Type B. A community market has nine physically distinct prize tickets: four worth 0 USD each, three worth 5 USD
each and two worth 12 USD each. Choose a subset of three tickets uniformly without replacement, so every subset of three physically
distinct tickets has the same chance. Ignore selection order. First add the face values. Then add one bonus of 7 USD if the selected
subset contains at least one 5 USD ticket and at least one 12 USD ticket; otherwise add no bonus. Finally, if this adjusted amount
exceeds 25 USD, pay only 25 USD; otherwise pay the adjusted amount. No other payments or deductions apply. Record the final
payout.

bonus and then the cap, aggregate equal final payouts, and derive and verify the PMF. Produce its table and labeled graph. [3]
