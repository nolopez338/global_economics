The evidence is consistent with nested intervals: whenever a narrower interval covers the simulated mean, its
wider partners also cover it. Within each simulated triple, the centre is that fresh sample’s mean; it can vary
between samples.
The observed rates describe this finite run and need not match the nominal levels exactly. Under the model,
about 90% , 95% ,and 99% oftherespectiveintervalscoverthefixedpopulationmeanovermanyrepetitions.
Higher confidence requires a wider interval from a given sample; it does not move the sample mean . Once
calculated, each interval either contains the fixed mean or misses it ; a confidence level is not a probability
assigned to that particular fixed interval containing the mean.
