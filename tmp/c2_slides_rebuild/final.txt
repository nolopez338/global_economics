levels
C2 Compares point and interval estimation and interprets confidence levels
through their long-run coverage meaning, distinguishing this from the probability
that a particular calculated interval contains the parameter.

Task 3 – What confidence means
Nominal 95% confidence describes the procedure’s long-run coverage: under the
model, about 95% of intervals from repeated fresh samples cover the fixed population
mean.

Each realized interval either contains that fixed parameter or misses it. Confidence is
not a probability assigned to an already calculated interval.
