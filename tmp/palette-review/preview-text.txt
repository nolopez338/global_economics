Terms and symbols                                                    Meaning in context

Population mean , µ                                                  The fixed population parameter being estimated.

Point estimate , x̄                                                  Sample mean and interval centre; for example, 60 units .

Population SD , σ ; sample size , n                                  σ describes individual observations; n counts sampled units. For example,
                                                                                                          p
                                                                     20 units and 100 give SE = 20 / 100 = 2 units .

Standard error , SE                                                  Spread of sample means across repeated samples, or its estimate; for example,
                                                                      2 units . Distinct from individual-observation spread.

Standard normal       critical value , z ∗                           Unitless multiplier selected by C ; 1.96 bounds the central area 0.95 between
                                                                     − z ∗ and z ∗ .

Margin of error , MoE                                                Distance from centre to an endpoint: MoE = z ∗ SE . For example,
                                                                      1.96 ( 2 ) = 3.92 units .

Endpoints , x̄ ± MoE                                                 Lower and upper bounds: 60 ± 3.92 gives 56.08 and 63.92 units .

Full width , 2 MoE                                                   Distance between endpoints, twice the margin of error; here 2( 3.92 ) = 7.84 units .

Confidence level , C                                                 Nominal long-run coverage is C ; α = 1 − C , using decimal C . For
significance level , α                                                C = 0.95 , α = 0.05 ; each tail has area α /2 = 0.025 . These settings do not
                                                                     come from observed coverage.

Observed coverage , rate                                             Fraction covering the fixed mean in a run; for example, 4/5 = 80% . Each interval
                                                                     covers or misses; this fraction can differ from C .
