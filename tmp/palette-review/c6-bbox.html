All data in this activity are hypothetical teaching models. Probabilities have no monetary units. Keep exact expressions where useful and round final probabilities to four decimal places. 1.1 Wait for a target number of sales A school enterprise needs three sales. It contacts visitors independently, with constant purchase probability p. Let T count all contacts up to and including the third sale. The target r = 3 successes is fixed, but the number of trials is random. Support is {3, 4, 5, . . .} contacts for 0 < p < 1. Check. Could the third sale occur on contact two? Suggested answer: No. One contact gives at most one success; T ≥ r. 1.2 Fix the final success For the third sale to occur on contact five, contact five must be a success.
