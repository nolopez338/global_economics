1.1 Weekly earnings for courier shifts (C4)
A courier cooperative draws a simple random sample of 64 distinct members from a roster of 2,400. Each
member contributes their total earnings for the same reference week. The sample mean is 410 thousand COP.
For this exercise take the population earnings variance to be known:σ2 = 6,400in squared thousand COP.
Assume the distribution has no extreme skew or outliers, so the sample mean has an approximately normal
sampling distribution at this sample size.
Questions/Tasks
