Outcomes, Support and Probability
Mass Functions
C1. Characterises discrete random variables in economic and financial contexts by
identifying their support, probability mass function and possible outcomes.
All data are hypothetical teaching models. Keep exact expressions where useful
and round final probabilities to four decimal places.
