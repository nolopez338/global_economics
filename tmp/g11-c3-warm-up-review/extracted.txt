Planning the school journey
A school surveys a random sample of 60 students about their journey times.
Their average is 30 minutes .

Using the same survey, two reports suggest ranges for the true average journey
time of all students:

Report A 28–32 minutes
Report B 24–36 minutes

Which report would you choose if your priority is to avoid leaving the
true average outside the range? Explain your reasoning.

Think and discuss: 60 seconds.
