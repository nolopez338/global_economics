Discrete Probability Distributions

Concepts, distributions, and criteria for discrete random variables and introductory investment decisions.

C1

Characterises discrete random variables in economic and financial contexts by identifying their support, probability mass function and possible outcomes.

C2

Interprets PMFs and CDFs by verifying their defining properties and using them to calculate point, interval, cumulative, and complementary probabilities in financial contexts.

C3

Models binary economic outcomes with Bernoulli random variables, interpreting success and failure in contexts such as default, gain, loss, or claim occurrence.

C4

Applies the binomial distribution to fixed-trial financial situations by verifying its conditions and calculating probabilities using the binomial probability formula \(P(X=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k}\).

C5

Models waiting-time situations with geometric distributions, calculating the probability of the first success occurring on a given trial in financial or business contexts.

C6

Applies the negative binomial distribution to situations that count the number of trials needed to obtain a fixed number of successes.

C7

Evaluates rare-event situations with the Poisson distribution, interpreting the parameter \(\lambda\) in contexts such as claims, arrivals, defects, or defaults within a fixed interval.

C8

Calculates and interprets the expected value and variance of discrete probability distributions as measures of return and risk in economic and financial contexts.

C9

Compares Bernoulli, binomial, negative binomial, geometric, and Poisson distributions by selecting the most appropriate model for a given financial-risk situation.

C10

Assesses investment alternatives for inclusion in a portfolio by comparing their discrete return distributions, expected returns, variances, and risk–return trade-offs, using the results to support investment portfolio decision-making.

Core discrete distributions summarize how to model single trials, repeated trials, and rare-event counts in this unit.

  • Bernoulli(p): Single trial with success probability p; mean p, variance p(1-p).
  • Binomial(n, p): Number of successes in n Bernoulli trials; PMF C(n, k)p^k(1-p)^{n-k}.
  • Geometric(p): Trials until first success; PMF (1-p)^{k-1}p; mean 1/p.
  • Poisson(λ): Counts of rare independent events in a fixed interval; PMF e^{-λ}λ^k / k!; mean = variance = λ.

For a discrete random variable X with PMF f(x), expectation is E[X] = Σ x·f(x) and variance is Var(X) = E[X^2] - (E[X])^2.

Example: For a fair die, E[X] = (1+2+3+4+5+6)/6 = 3.5 and Var(X) = E[X^2] - (E[X])^2 = (1^2+...+6^2)/6 − 3.5^2 ≈ 2.92.
