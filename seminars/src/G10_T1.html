Understanding Needs & Opportunities

Identifying needs, understanding users, exploring contexts, and defining an evidence-based innovation challenge.

C1

Recognizes needs, discomforts, or opportunities for improvement within the school or nearby community.

C2

Observes behaviors, spaces, users, or situations to better understand a need.

C3

Listens to potential users through conversations, guided questions, or empathy exercises.

C4

Differentiates a real need from an anticipated solution idea.

C5

Organizes contextual information using maps, user profiles, lists of findings, or simple diagrams.

C6

Prioritizes an innovation opportunity considering relevance, feasibility of action, and expected benefit.

C7

Defines a clear, concrete, and understandable innovation challenge for the group.

C8

Relates the challenge to knowledge from design, communication, economics, technology, citizenship, or another relevant discipline.

C9

Participates in initial ideation activities with openness to diverse proposals.

C10

Recognizes the importance of understanding the user before proposing a solution.

This term introduces an evidence-based approach to identifying opportunities for innovation. Students begin by exploring their school and nearby community, observing contexts, listening to potential users, organizing findings, and defining an innovation challenge.

The central principle is to understand a real need before proposing a solution.
