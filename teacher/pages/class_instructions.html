Before we begin

READ THE INSTRUCTIONS CAREFULLY.

Attendance

In order to begin, at least half of the class must be in the room.

Take your seat

All students present must be seated and follow the Classroom Rules about what is allowed and not allowed on their desks.

Allowed on your desk and in the classroom

  • Pencil case
  • Calculator
  • Notebook
  • Backpack
  • Loose papers
  • Activity guide

Not allowed on your desk or in the classroom

  • Food
  • Cellphones
  • Card games
  1. 1

    At the start of every class

    Identify today’s monitor

    One monitor per occupied row: the student in the last occupied seat, farthest from the board when facing it.

    Check today’s seating every class. This responsibility follows the seat; it is not permanent or random.

  2. 2

    Before class ends

    Check alignment

    As monitor, check that every desk and chair in your row is neatly aligned.

  3. 3

    Before class ends

    Check cleanliness

    Check the whole row, including the floor around and under every desk.

    No litter remains anywhere along the row.

Everyone tidies their own space. The row monitor checks the whole row.
