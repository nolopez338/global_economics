Methodology, definitions & assumptions

We use the populated sheets and task-specific notes rather than the older template introduction. Blanks remain unavailable; genuine zeros and negative enjoyment scores remain observations. Recorded anomalies (including a completed age of 0 and inconsistent first-phone age) are preserved. No plausibility exclusions are applied.

Birth weekday (q01_birth_weekday) is available in the numerical explorer as Monday=1, Tuesday=2, Wednesday=3, Thursday=4, Friday=5, Saturday=6, Sunday=7. Numeric range filters and analysis exports use these codes; the category view also shows the weekday names. Weekdays form a cycle, so numerical summaries describe the codes.

Histogram intervals are lower-inclusive and upper-exclusive, with the final upper endpoint included. Comparisons share edges and percentages normalize within each group’s usable n. Automatic binning uses width 2 × IQR / ∛n (Freedman–Diaconis); zero IQR or n < 4 falls back to Sturges’ number of bins. Constant data uses a width of 1. Bins are limited to 2,000 for browser responsiveness. Quartiles use linear interpolation at (n−1)p; standard deviation uses n−1 and requires two observations.

Quality filters inspect only the current answer. Derived measurements have no invented answer status: all documented source dependencies must pass the selected statuses. Their source statuses and derived issues are shown in details. “Unavailable / excluded” includes blank or unusable values and values excluded by the quality filter; these counts are broken out below the cards. New workbook statuses appear automatically.

Complete rankings have a unique 1, 2, and 3 with no invalid or duplicate rank codes; blank unselected options are allowed. Consistent partial rankings retain known choices. Zero means explicitly unranked in original rank fields; blank means uncertain/unavailable. Selected and points fields use the workbook’s documented partial-ranking convention. Rank-specific denominators count respondents with that usable rank; top-three and split-rank percentages use eligible respondents and can total above 100%. Points (3, 2, 1) are preference scores, never probabilities.

A zero homework denominator leaves the phone/homework ratio unavailable. The phone-minus-homework difference can remain valid. Books have no reporting period. Exercise minutes are a lower bound from qualifying days; nonqualifying days can include exercise. First-phone elapsed years are approximate. Annual phone totals and weekly travel are estimates/projections. No ages or birthday values are recalculated using the current date. Scatterplots use pairwise-complete observations and describe associations without implying causation.

Workbook text is rendered as text, and uploaded files stay in this browser. No workbook contents are sent to external services. CSV text that could execute as a spreadsheet formula is prefixed with an apostrophe for safe export.
