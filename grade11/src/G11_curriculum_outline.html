Grade 11 · Curriculum Outline

3 Terms outline

  1. Step 1

    Grade 11 · Term 1

    C1 characterises continuous random variables through their PDFs, CDFs, and support; C2 applies uniform and triangular densities; C3 uses circular-arc densities; C4 assesses exponential waiting-time models; C5 calculates and interprets continuous-distribution means and variances; C6 compares simulated relative frequencies with theoretical probabilities; C7 compares sample means and variances across sample sizes with their theoretical values; C8 uses Student’s t tables, degrees of freedom, and one- or two-tailed areas; C9 selects and justifies normal or Student’s t methods according to whether the population standard deviation is known or estimated; and C10 integrates these methods with portfolio-risk measures in an economic or financial investigation, communicating assumptions, limitations, and evidence-based conclusions.

  2. Step 2

    Grade 11 · Term 2

    Confidence and interval estimation: population parameters versus sample statistics, point and interval estimates, confidence levels, critical values, standard errors, margins of error, confidence intervals for means and proportions, sample-size decisions, t-intervals, finite population correction, interpretation, precision, and limitations.

  3. Step 3

    Grade 11 · Term 3

    Hypothesis testing for economics and finance: test elements, null and alternative hypotheses, tail direction, Type I and Type II errors, significance and power, Z-tests and t-tests for means and proportions, paired and independent samples, automated calculations, visualisation, and evidence-based research communication.
