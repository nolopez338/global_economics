Build each pdf, then find probabilities as areas

Grade 11 · Term 1 · C2

Five density problems, solved with area

  1. Method

    Equation of a line through two points

    For \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\), first find the slope and intercept:

    \[ m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}, \qquad b=y_1-mx_1, \qquad y=mx+b. \]

    The equivalent point-slope form is

    \[ y-y_1=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}(x-x_1), \qquad x_1\ne x_2. \]
    If \(x_1=x_2\), the line is vertical: \(x=x_1\).

  2. Problem 1

    Uniform distribution

    TransMilenio after class

    A student waits for TransMilenio after class. Let \(T\) be the waiting time, in minutes.

    • Support: \([0,4]\).
    • Every waiting time is equally likely.
    Find: the complete pdf, \(P(T>3)\), and \(P(1\le T\le3)\).

  3. Solution 1

    Uniform distribution: rectangles

    1

    Support and shape

    \([0,4]\); one constant-height rectangle.

    2

    Total area \(=1\)

    \[1=4h,\qquad h=\frac14.\]
    3

    Straight-line rule

    No line equation is needed: the density is constant.

    4

    Complete pdf

    \[ f_T(t)=\begin{cases} \dfrac14,&0\le t\le4,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]
    5

    Tail rectangle

    \[P(T>3)=(4-3)\frac14=\frac14.\]
    6

    Interval rectangle

    \[P(1\le T\le3)=(3-1)\frac14=\frac12.\]

  4. Problem 2

    Linear distribution

    Restaurant pickup in Chapinero

    A young delivery rider waits for a restaurant order in Chapinero. Let \(W\) be the waiting time, in minutes.

    • Support: \([0,2]\).
    • The density increases linearly.
    • Its height at \(2\) minutes is three times its height at \(0\).
    Find: the complete pdf, \(P(W>1)\), and \(P(0\le W\le1)\).

  5. Solution 2

    Linear distribution: trapezoids

    1

    Support and shape

    \([0,2]\); one rising line with positive endpoint heights \(h\) and \(3h\).

    2

    Total area \(=1\)

    \[f_W(0)=h,\quad f_W(2)=3h\]
    \[1=\frac12(h+3h)(2)=4h,\quad h=\frac14.\]
    3

    Line through two points

    \[ \left(0,\frac14\right),\ \left(2,\frac34\right):\qquad m=\frac{\frac34-\frac14}{2-0}=\frac14, \qquad b=\frac14, \qquad f_W(w)=\frac w4+\frac14=\frac{w+1}{4}. \]
    4

    Complete pdf

    \[ f_W(w)=\begin{cases} \dfrac{w+1}{4},&0\le w\le2,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]
    5

    Tail trapezoid

    \[P(W>1)=\frac12\left(\frac12+\frac34\right)(1)=\frac58.\]
    6

    Interval trapezoid

    \[P(0\le W\le1)=\frac12\left(\frac14+\frac12\right)(1)=\frac38.\]

  6. Problem 3

    Triangular distribution

    Sunday Ciclovía ride

    A young adult records the length of a Sunday Ciclovía ride. Let \(C\) be the ride length, in hours.

    • Support: \([0,4]\).
    • The triangular density is \(0\) at \(0\) and \(4\).
    • Its mode is \(2\).
    Find: the complete pdf, \(P(C>3)\), and \(P(1\le C\le3)\).

  7. Solution 3

    Triangular distribution: subtract the tails

    1

    Support and shape

    \([0,4]\); a triangle with zero endpoints and mode \(2\).

    2

    Total area \(=1\)

    \[1=\frac12(4)H,\qquad H=\frac12.\]
    3

    Lines through endpoint pairs

    \[ (0,0),(2,\tfrac12):\ m_1=\frac{\frac12-0}{2-0}=\frac14,\ b_1=0; \qquad (2,\tfrac12),(4,0):\ m_2=\frac{0-\frac12}{4-2}=-\frac14,\ b_2=1. \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac c4,\ \frac{4-c}{4}. \]
    4

    Complete pdf

    \[ f_C(c)=\begin{cases} \dfrac c4,&0\le c\le2,\\ \dfrac{4-c}{4},&2<c\le4,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]
    5

    Tail triangle

    \[P(C>3)=\frac12(1)\left(\frac14\right)=\frac18.\]
    6

    Interval by subtracting tails

    \[P(1\le C\le3)=1-\frac18-\frac18=\frac34.\]

  8. Problem 4

    Piecewise-uniform distribution

    Collecting printed notes

    A university student waits to collect printed notes near campus. Let \(P\) be the waiting time, in minutes.

    • Support: \([0,3]\).
    • The density is constant on \([0,1)\) and on \([1,3]\).
    • The first-minute density is twice the later density.
    Find: the complete pdf, \(P(P>2)\), and \(P(0\le P\le2)\).

  9. Solution 4

    Piecewise uniform: add rectangles

    1

    Support and shape

    \([0,3]\); two constant sections split at \(1\).

    2

    Total area \(=1\)

    \[1=(1)(2k)+(2)(k)=4k,\qquad k=\frac14.\]
    3

    Straight-line rule

    No line equation is needed: both sections are constant.

    4

    Complete pdf

    \[ f_P(p)=\begin{cases} \dfrac12,&0\le p<1,\\ \dfrac14,&1\le p\le3,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]
    5

    Tail rectangle

    \[P(P>2)=(3-2)\frac14=\frac14.\]
    6

    Interval rectangles

    \[P(0\le P\le2)=(1)\frac12+(2-1)\frac14=\frac34.\]

  10. Problem 5

    Piecewise-linear distribution

    Technology meetup in Bogotá

    A young adult attends a technology meetup in Bogotá. Let \(M\) be the visit length, in hours.

    • Support: \([0,3]\).
    • The density rises linearly from \(0\) at \(M=0\) to an unknown height \(H\) at \(M=1\).
    • It stays constant to \(M=2\), then falls linearly to \(0\) at \(M=3\).
    Find: the complete pdf, \(P(M>2)\), and \(P(1\le M\le2)\).

  11. Solution 5

    Piecewise linear: triangles and a rectangle

    1

    Support and shape

    \([0,3]\); rise on \([0,1]\), plateau on \((1,2]\), fall on \((2,3]\).

    2

    Total area \(=1\)

    \[1=\frac12(1)H+(1)H+\frac12(1)H=2H,\qquad H=\frac12.\]
    3

    Lines through endpoint pairs

    \[ (0,0),(1,\tfrac12):\ m_1=\frac{\frac12-0}{1-0}=\frac12,\ b_1=0; \qquad (2,\tfrac12),(3,0):\ m_2=\frac{0-\frac12}{3-2}=-\frac12,\ b_2=\frac32. \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac m2,\ \frac{3-m}{2}. \]
    4

    Complete pdf

    \[ f_M(m)=\begin{cases} \dfrac m2,&0\le m\le1,\\ \dfrac12,&1<m\le2,\\ \dfrac{3-m}{2},&2<m\le3,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]
    5

    Tail triangle

    \[P(M>2)=\frac12(1)\left(\frac12\right)=\frac14.\]
    6

    Interval rectangle

    \[P(1\le M\le2)=(1)\left(\frac12\right)=\frac12.\]
