A LITTLE GUIDANCE

From pieces to possibilities.

Place a piece
Drag a material onto the working plane, or click a material then click the workspace. Aim within 192 CSS screen pixels of a free compatible connection to preview the piece aligned there. Cyan markers and a pointer guide identify the proposed joint, and the translucent piece shows its resulting position. This range stays the same at every camera angle and zoom. Move closer to another port to switch targets; a small 12-pixel switching margin keeps the preview steady. Move beyond the 192-pixel range for free placement on the working plane. Click or release the drag to commit the geometry shown. Turn off “Snap compatible ports” for free placement everywhere. Q/E and the labeled rotation buttons work as soon as you choose a material, before moving the pointer. In free placement, Q rotates around world Y and E around world X. During a connection preview, rotations explore all compatible source ports and choose the closest aligned orientation, preserving rotation around the joint axis where possible. Pointer movement keeps your requested orientation; moving away restores it for free placement. Equally close source ports use their endpoint order. Rotation keys also work during catalog dragging when the browser delivers them. Choose 15°, 30°, 45°, 90° or a custom positive rotation increment in Options; this browser remembers it. The increment also applies to the X/Y/Z buttons. Escape cancels.
Look around
In normal selection mode, left drag pans, right drag orbits, and scroll zooms. Middle drag also pans. A small movement threshold separates clicks from drags. Camera presets give perspective, top, front and side views. Frame or F fits your selection, or the whole design when empty.
Select & transform
Click selects; Shift-click toggles. Marquee tool drags a screen rectangle around piece centres. Double-click a piece to select it and enter Piece movement mode. Left-drag a piece on a plane parallel to the working plane through its current position, without jumping to the plane offset. Release to snap and stay in movement mode. Escape or Select exits; Escape during a drag restores its starting geometry. The mode banner and teal/amber highlights identify what moves. Move (G) and Rotate (R) expose axis handles; the movement mode also keeps translation handles available. Q/E rotate the preview or entire connected selection rigidly, preserving the arrangement of multiple selected pieces. Each completed drag or discrete rotation is one undoable action. Shortcuts are ignored while typing in form fields. Numeric fields set world position and XYZ Euler rotation. Connected pieces always move together, including the amber-highlighted companions.
Connect
Bring a free tube end near a free fitting socket. Cyan markers and a translucent preview show the proposed snap. Release to connect; the moving assembly aligns rigidly. You can also select two ports in the inspector. A tube seats 20 mm inside a socket. Closed frames must align exactly to close.
Detach a joint
Use Joints mode and click a cyan joint marker, or select a joint in the inspector. Detach removes just that edge; geometry stays in place. Alternate paths keep an assembly joined.
Change a cut length
Use the unit button beside Tube cut length to cycle length units throughout the workspace. The number and slider stay in sync. A connected end stays fixed; a second branch translates when free to do so. A closed loop locks length until you detach a joint. Free tubes resize about their centres.
Copy & history
Ctrl/Cmd+C and V preserve selected internal joints. External joints are omitted. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes. Ctrl/Cmd+A selects all; Delete removes the selection. Escape clears selection or cancels placement / a transform.
Keep your work
Changes auto-save on this browser. Save JSON exports a portable file; Load JSON validates it before replacing your design. New and sample designs ask before replacing unexported work.
Download PDF
Download PDF saves the complete committed design locally as pvc-design.pdf: three A4 portrait pages with a materials list and eight labeled views. Cut lengths use the selected length unit and include socket insertion. Front is viewed from +Z, right from +X, and top from +Y; top points toward -Z. Pending placement and editing overlays are excluded. Large lists use two columns; if the table cannot fit legibly on one page, export reports an error. PDF export does not mark your JSON backup as saved.

This is a geometric construction planner. It does not certify load capacity, joint strength, collisions, or manufacturing tolerances.
