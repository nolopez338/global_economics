A LITTLE GUIDANCE

From pieces to possibilities.

Place a piece
Drag a material onto the working plane, or click a material then click the workspace. Preview before placing; Q turns it 90°. Escape cancels.
Look around
Right drag orbits; middle drag pans; scroll zooms. Camera presets give perspective, top, front and side views. Frame or F fits your selection, or the whole design when empty.
Select & transform
Click selects; Shift-click toggles. Marquee tool drags a screen rectangle around piece centres. Move (G) and Rotate (R) expose axis handles. Numeric fields set world position and XYZ Euler rotation. Connected pieces always move together, including the amber-highlighted companions.
Connect
Bring a free tube end near a free fitting socket. Cyan markers and a translucent preview show the proposed snap. Release to connect; the moving assembly aligns rigidly. You can also select two ports in the inspector. A tube seats 20 mm inside a socket. Closed frames must align exactly to close.
Detach a joint
Use Joints mode and click a cyan joint marker, or select a joint in the inspector. Detach removes just that edge; geometry stays in place. Alternate paths keep an assembly joined.
Change a cut length
The number and slider stay in sync. A connected end stays fixed; a second branch translates when free to do so. A closed loop locks length until you detach a joint. Free tubes resize about their centres.
Copy & history
Ctrl/Cmd+C and V preserve selected internal joints. External joints are omitted. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes. Ctrl/Cmd+A selects all; Delete removes the selection. Escape clears selection or cancels placement / a transform.
Keep your work
Changes auto-save on this browser. Save JSON exports a portable file; Load JSON validates it before replacing your design. New and sample designs ask before replacing unexported work.

This is a geometric construction planner. It does not certify load capacity, joint strength, collisions, or manufacturing tolerances.
