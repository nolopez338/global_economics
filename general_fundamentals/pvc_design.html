A LITTLE GUIDANCE

From pieces to possibilities.

Place a Person scale reference
Person is a reference, excluded from PVC materials and structural-template dimensions. Place multiple people by catalog click or drag. Adult defaults to 1,800 mm with inclusive limits 1,400–2,200 mm; Child uses 600–1,700 mm. These are configurable application limits shared by Man and Woman. Height scales width and depth proportionally, measured from the soles at the local origin to the crown. Category changes preserve height where possible and clamp otherwise, with a brief announcement. The inspector shares the mm/cm/m units, uses a 10 mm base increment, previews edits live and commits one undo action on release, Enter, blur or selection change. Escape restores the starting height. People have no ports, snapping or collision restrictions; overlap freely. They move only when explicitly selected, including in mixed selections. JSON and autosave retain them; PDF views include them while its materials list excludes them.
Standard components only
Straight tube, straight coupling, 90° and 45° elbows, three-port tee, four-port cross and three-way corner (three perpendicular sockets). Fitting socket counts, positions, directions and compatibility are fixed. Only tube cut length varies among PVC components; Person is a separate scale reference. Custom rotation increments remain a placement setting. Unsupported components or socket overrides are rejected in JSON, autosave, paste and history without partially loading the design.
Place a piece
Drag a material onto the working plane, or click a material then click the workspace. Aim within 192 CSS screen pixels of a free compatible connection to preview the piece aligned there. Cyan markers and a pointer guide identify the proposed joint, and the translucent piece shows its resulting position. This range stays the same at every camera angle and zoom. Move closer to another port to switch targets; a switching margin of up to 12 pixels keeps the preview steady. Move beyond the 192-pixel range for free placement on the working plane. Click or release the drag to commit the geometry shown. Turn off “Snap compatible ports” for free placement everywhere. Q/E and the labeled rotation buttons work as soon as you choose a material, before moving the pointer. In free placement, Q rotates around world Y and E around world X. During a connection preview, rotations explore all compatible source ports and choose the closest aligned orientation, preserving rotation around the joint axis where possible. Pointer movement keeps your requested orientation; moving away restores it for free placement. Equally close source ports use their endpoint order. Rotation keys also work during catalog dragging when the browser delivers them. Choose 15°, 30°, 45°, 90° or a custom positive rotation increment in Options; this browser remembers it. The increment also applies to the X/Y/Z buttons. Escape cancels.
Measure the selection
The small box button beside Frame cycles Off, World-aligned, and Approximate minimum-volume oriented bounds. It encloses only the pieces explicitly selected, including their rendered thickness, fitting bodies and complete Person geometry. Select connected assembly to measure an entire assembly. World dimensions are X/Y/Z; oriented dimensions are local L/W/H. The three labels use the current mm/cm/m unit. Oriented search is a numerical approximation with a target angular resolution of 0.000573 degrees; a global minimum and global volume-error bound are not guaranteed. The approximately-equal symbol identifies oriented measurements. Live edits show a containing preview, then refine after the edit settles. Measurements are presentation only and do not alter history, saved designs or exports.
Look around
In normal selection mode, left drag pans, right drag orbits, and scroll zooms. Middle drag also pans. A small movement threshold separates clicks from drags. Camera presets give perspective, top, front and side views. Frame or F fits your selection, or the whole design when empty.
Select & transform
Click selects; Shift-click toggles. Marquee tool drags a screen rectangle around piece centres. Double-click a piece to select it and enter Piece movement mode. Left-drag a piece on a plane parallel to the working plane through its current position, without jumping to the plane offset. Release to snap and stay in movement mode. Escape or Select exits; Escape during a drag restores its starting geometry. The mode banner and teal/amber highlights identify what moves. Move (G) and Rotate (R) expose axis handles; the movement mode also keeps translation handles available. Rotation rings use magnetic snapping around the active world axis, independently of “Snap to 25 mm grid”: 0°/360°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°, 120°, 135°, 150°, 180°, 210°, 225°, 240°, 270°, 300°, 315° and 330°, repeated in either direction on every turn. Angles are measured from the start of the drag. Enter within 3° to catch a target; it holds until your pointer angle moves more than 5° away. Keep dragging beyond it to ease back into free rotation and choose any angle. A released target can catch again on a later approach. Groups and connected companions rotate rigidly about one shared pivot. Q/E rotate the preview or entire connected selection rigidly, preserving the arrangement of multiple selected pieces. Each completed drag or discrete rotation is one undoable action. Shortcuts are ignored while typing in form fields. Numeric fields set exact world position and XYZ Euler rotation without magnetic snapping. Rotation increment applies to Q/E and rotation buttons, independently of ring dragging. Connected pieces always move together, including the amber-highlighted companions.
Connect
Bring a free tube end near a free fitting socket. Cyan markers and a translucent preview show the proposed snap. Release to connect; the moving assembly aligns rigidly. You can also select two ports in the inspector. A tube seats 20 mm inside a socket. Closed frames must align exactly to close.
Detach a joint
Use Joints mode and click a cyan joint marker, or select a joint in the inspector. Detach removes just that edge; geometry stays in place. Alternate paths keep an assembly joined.
Change a cut length
Use the unit button beside Tube cut length to cycle length units throughout the workspace. The number and slider stay in sync. A connected end stays fixed; a second branch translates when free to do so. A closed loop locks single-tube length until you detach a joint. Free tubes resize about their centres.
Change parallel tubes together
Shift-click or marquee every tube to resize, including tubes with reversed endpoint directions. “Change selected tube lengths” adds the same signed adjustment to each starting cut, preserving unequal lengths. All selected pieces must be tubes and their connected geometry must permit the same adjustment without changing any other cut. The inspector explains blocked selections. Each remaining connected section translates rigidly; its fittings never rotate. For each connected group, the attached section with the lowest tube endpoint along the tube axis oriented toward its positive dominant world axis stays fixed (ties use stable piece IDs). Completely free tubes keep their centres. The inspector names the anchor section. Controls start at zero after each completed edit and share the current mm/cm/m unit. The range keeps every cut within 80–3,000 mm. Slider drags and numeric edits preview from their starting snapshot; release, Enter or leaving the field commits one undo action. Escape while editing restores the starting geometry. Selection changes finish the current valid preview. Arrow keys adjust the slider; Home/End select its limits. In Template 7, select the four left-bay horizontal rails: centres X = −350 mm, Y = 21 or 821 mm, Z = −300 or 300 mm. All four cuts start at 650 mm; +100 mm makes each 750 mm, keeping all joints and other cuts intact. The left support section stays fixed and the centre/right portion moves +100 mm along world X.
Copy & history
Ctrl/Cmd+C and V preserve selected internal joints. External joints are omitted. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes. Ctrl/Cmd+A selects all; Delete removes the selection. Escape clears selection or cancels placement / a transform.
Keep your work
Changes auto-save on this browser. Save JSON exports a portable file; Load JSON validates it before replacing your design. New and the ten structural templates ask before replacing unexported work. New clears the design; all template options contain structures.
Download PDF
Download PDF saves the complete committed design locally as pvc-design.pdf: three A4 portrait pages with a materials list and eight labeled views. Cut lengths use the selected length unit and include socket insertion. Front is viewed from +Z, right from +X, and top from +Y; top points toward -Z. Pending placement and editing overlays are excluded. Large lists use two columns; if the table cannot fit legibly on one page, export reports an error. PDF export does not mark your JSON backup as saved.
