Build the interval for the mean

Displayed results are rounded; calculations retain full precision. Fitted generator σ is a plug-in value, with no finite-population correction. This z-interval is a model-based approximation; nominal coverage can be inaccurate for small samples or nonnormal mixtures. At zero variance, intervals collapse and nominal coverage has no useful interpretation. The target is the model mean μ, not the empirical mean of this displayed collection.

What changes? What stays fixed?

  • At fixed confidence, increasing n reduces SE and margin of error.
  • For the same sample, increasing confidence widens the interval.
  • Drawing another sample changes x̄ and the interval. Each calculated interval either covers the fixed μ or it does not.

Why 95%? Try independent studies

Each study generates n fresh independent values from the current fitted generator. These studies leave your displayed observations and selected sample unchanged. The experiment uses your current n and confidence.

Solid intervals labeled “covers” include μ; dashed intervals labeled “misses” do not. Observed coverage varies and need not approach nominal confidence monotonically. Changing n, confidence, or dataset selection resets this experiment.
