		\SubsectionBox{1. Local Food Market Stall \score{C1-\sml{1} \hspace{2mm} C2-\sml{2}}}
		A local food market company is planning its stall offering for an upcoming market period. Characterize the key elements of the decision-making situation and construct the payoff table.
		
		\SubsectionBox{2. Small Bakery Production \score{C1-\sml{1} \hspace{2mm} C2-\sml{3}}}
		A small bakery is planning its production capacity for an upcoming sales period. Characterize the key elements of the decision-making situation and construct the payoff table.
		
		\SubsectionBox{3. Urban Meal Subscription \score{C1-\sml{2} \hspace{2mm} C2-\sml{5}}}
		An urban meal subscription company must choose between a basic meal plan and a premium meal plan before customer interest is known. Characterize the key elements of the decision-making situation and construct the payoff table.
		
		\SubsectionBox{4. Regional Bookstore Expansion \score{C1-\sml{1} \hspace{2mm} C3-\sml{2}}}
		A regional bookstore must decide whether to open a children's section or maintain its current product selection before customer traffic is known. Characterize the key elements of the decision-making situation and construct the payoff table.
		
		\SubsectionBox{5. Greenhouse Crop Production \score{C1-\sml{1} \hspace{2mm} C3-\sml{3}}}
		A greenhouse farm is planning its production for an upcoming growing season. Characterize the key elements of the decision-making situation and construct the payoff table.
		
		\SubsectionBox{6. Fitness Center Membership \score{C1-\sml{2} \hspace{2mm} C3-\sml{5}}}
		A fitness center must choose between a basic membership plan and a premium membership plan before monthly demand is known. Characterize the key elements of the decision-making situation and construct the payoff table.
		
		\SubsectionBox{7. Restaurant Expansion Strategy \score{C4-\sml{2}}}
		A restaurant company must choose a strategy for expanding into a new location. Customer demand could be high, medium, or low. What would the optimistic decision be? What would the conservative decision be? Justify your answer.
		
		\SubsectionBox{8. Hotel Development Strategy \score{C4-\sml{2}}}
		A hotel company must choose a strategy for developing a new property. Tourist demand could be high, medium, or low. What would the optimistic decision be? What would the conservative decision be? Justify your answer.
		
		\SubsectionBox{9. Agricultural Technology Investment \score{C4-\sml{2}}}
		An agricultural company must choose a strategy for introducing a new automated farming system. Market conditions could be highly favourable, favourable, stable, or unfavourable. What would the optimistic decision be? What would the conservative decision be? Justify your answer.
