# Directory Summary

## Purpose

Provides Grade 10, Term 1 LaTeX classroom activities and worked solutions for decision modelling, payoff construction, criterion-based recommendations, probability sensitivity, and staged filtering.

## Files

| File | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| [`G10_T1_C1C2C3C4_motivation-content.tex`](G10_T1_C1C2C3C4_motivation-content.tex) | LaTeX | **C1–C4.** Explains abstraction through café, farm, and investment contexts, connecting decision elements, payoff tables, revenue–cost models, Maximax, Maximin, and contextual interpretation in a standalone exposition. |
| [`G10_T1_C1C2C3C4_motivation.tex`](G10_T1_C1C2C3C4_motivation.tex) | LaTeX | **C1–C4.** Presents the abstraction exposition with evaluated criteria, a table of contents, and linked subsections introducing the decision-modelling workflow. |
| [`G10_T1_L1_C1C2_classroom_activity.tex`](G10_T1_L1_C1C2_classroom_activity.tex) | LaTeX | **C1–C2.** Develops decision interpretation and payoff-table construction through six worked contexts, progressing from direct profits to alternative- and state-based variable/fixed components; its source title refers to Grade 11. |
| [`G10_T1_L1_C1C3_classroom_activity.tex`](G10_T1_L1_C1C3_classroom_activity.tex) | LaTeX | **C1 and C3.** Calculates revenue and cost independently, then constructs profit tables in four worked contexts: café investment, custom printing, produce subscriptions, and parcel delivery. |
| [`G10_T1_L1_C4_classroom_activity.tex`](G10_T1_L1_C4_classroom_activity.tex) | LaTeX | **C4.** Develops Maximax and Maximin through a solved coffee-shop example, guided comparisons, and independent practice using row maxima, row minima, and optimistic or conservative recommendations. |
| [`G10_T1_L1_C5_classroom_activity.tex`](G10_T1_L1_C5_classroom_activity.tex) | LaTeX | **C5.** Develops statewise opportunity loss and Minimax Regret through a solved clothing-store example, scaffolded tables, independent business problems, and subsequent worked analyses; its learning-criterion paragraph labels this content C4. |
| [`G10_T1_L1_C6C7_classroom_activity.tex`](G10_T1_L1_C6C7_classroom_activity.tex) | LaTeX | **C6–C7.** Develops probability-weighted Expected Monetary Value and Bayes selection, then expected-value functions, indifference thresholds, and probability sensitivity through worked examples and business practice. |
| [`G10_T1_L3_C8_classroom_activity.tex`](G10_T1_L3_C8_classroom_activity.tex) | LaTeX | **C8.** Compares Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes in five problems addressing information needs, risk orientations, ties, probability changes, subsidy thresholds, and eligibility constraints, with worked solutions. |
| [`G10_T1_L3_C9_classroom_activity.tex`](G10_T1_L3_C9_classroom_activity.tex) | LaTeX | **C9.** Develops staged filtering with fixed supplied statistics, permanent eliminations, cutoff ties, numerical survivor tables, and matching decision trees; the four contexts select B₂, X₅, G₅, and Y₅. |
