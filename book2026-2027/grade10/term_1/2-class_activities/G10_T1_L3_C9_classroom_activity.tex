\begin{center}{\large\bfseries Grade 10 -- Term 1: C9 Classroom Activity}\end{center}

\textbf{Goal:} Construct and interpret decision trees for staged filtering.
Use supplied summaries (thousand USD).
Maximax ranks Max Payoff highest first; Maximin ranks Min Payoff highest first;
Minimax Regret ranks Max Regret lowest first; EMV ranks EMV highest first.
Compare only survivors; eliminated alternatives never return. Keep all ties at each cutoff; report final ties.
All statistics stay fixed, including regrets based on all six original alternatives.

A filter tree branches at each stage to survivors and eliminated alternatives; label each split with its rule.
Use your notebook for full tables, trees, and extra working.
