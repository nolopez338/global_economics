\textbf{Goal:} Compare decision criteria and solve numerical conditions that change a recommendation.
All profits and monetary parameters are in thousand US dollars; larger profits are better. Use the supplied summaries for Task 1. For Task 2, show your inequality, boundary ties, updated calculations, and numerical justification. Unless stated otherwise, probabilities and other payoffs stay unchanged. Compute regrets using eligible alternatives only. Do not assume missing probabilities.
