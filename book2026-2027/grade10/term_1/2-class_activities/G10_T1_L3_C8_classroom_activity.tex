\subsection*{1. Cold-storage cooperative}
\textbf{Task 1.} Maximax: $A_1$ (row maximum 96); Maximin: $A_3$ (row minimum 34); Minimax Regret: $A_2$ (maximum regret 24); EMV/Bayes: $A_2$ (EMV 56.4).

\textbf{Task 2.} $A_1$ fails because $-12<18$; $A_2,A_3$ remain eligible for every allowed $s$. Since $18+s\leq42<60$, the relevant minima are $18+s$ and 34. Thus $18+s>34\iff s>16$, so the smallest integer is $s=17$ (\$17,000). At $s=16$, Maximin ties at 34. At $s=17$, eligible state bests are $(72,60,35)$; regret rows are $(0,0,0)$ and $(24,18,1)$. Maximax changes through eligibility and Maximin changes through the grant.

\subsection*{2. Community laundry equipment}
\textbf{Task 1.} Maximax: $A_1$ (row maximum 120); Maximin: $A_3$ (row minimum 42); Minimax Regret: $A_1,A_2$ (maximum regret 36); EMV/Bayes: $A_1$ (EMV 70.8).

\textbf{Task 2.} The original EMVs are 70.8, 66, and 49.2. Since the probabilities sum to one, $EMV(A_1)=70.8-f$. Eligibility requires $f\leq6.8$, so $A_1,A_2$ qualify throughout $0\leq f\leq6$; $A_3$ never qualifies. Bayes changes when $66>70.8-f\iff f>4.8$. The boundary is a tie at $f=4.8$; the smallest allowed integer is $f=5$ (\$5,000). State bests at 5 are $(115,66,30)$, giving regret rows $(0,17,29)$ and $(31,0,0)$. Bayes changes to $A_2$; Maximin changes because $A_3$ is excluded.

\textbf{Task 2.} $E_1=8+84p$, $E_2=38+18p$, $E_3=42+2p$. Hence $E_1>E_2\iff p>5/11$ and $E_1>E_3\iff p>17/41$. Also $E_2>E_3\iff p>1/4$, true throughout the allowed range. Only Bayes changes: payoffs, extrema, and regrets are unchanged.
