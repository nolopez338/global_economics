		\section*{Learning Criterion}
		
		C4: Applies the Maximax and Maximin criteria to make optimistic or
		conservative decisions without probabilities, selecting the alternative
		with the highest possible payoff or the best worst-case outcome.
		
		% ============================================================
		% SUMMARY
		% ============================================================
		
		\section{Maximax and Maximin}
		
		When probabilities are unavailable, decisions can be made by comparing
		the payoffs for each alternative under each possible state of nature.
		
		\begin{center}
			\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
			\small
			\begin{tabular}{p{0.20\columnwidth}p{0.34\columnwidth}p{0.34\columnwidth}}
				\toprule
				& \textbf{Maximax} & \textbf{Maximin} \\
				\midrule
				Focus & Best possible payoff & Worst possible payoff \\
				Attitude & Optimistic & Conservative \\
				First step & Find the maximum in each row & Find the minimum in each row \\
				Final step & Choose the largest maximum & Choose the largest minimum \\
				\bottomrule
			\end{tabular}
		\end{center}
		
		\[
		\boxed{
			\begin{gathered}M_i=\max_j P_{ay}(A_i,S_j)\\ A^*_{\mathrm{Maximax}}=\arg\max_i M_i\end{gathered}
		}
		\]
		
		\[
		\boxed{
			\begin{gathered}m_i=\min_j P_{ay}(A_i,S_j)\\ A^*_{\mathrm{Maximin}}=\arg\max_i m_i\end{gathered}
		}
		\]
		

        In every problem, choose exactly one alternative before the external
        state is known. The alternatives are mutually exclusive; no state
        probabilities are available. All entries are profits in thousand USD,
        with higher values better (negative entries are losses).
        Use the supplied payoffs directly.

        Identify alternatives $A_i$ and states $S_j$. Find each row maximum
        and minimum, then choose the largest maximum for Maximax and the
        largest minimum for Maximin. Complete the summary where provided.

		\columnbreak
		\section{Solved Example: Boat Tours}

A boat operator chooses open-sea tours or harbor tours before next month's weather is known. The month will be calm or stormy. All payoffs are profits in thousand USD.

		\begin{center}
			\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
			\small
			\begin{tabular}{lcc}
				\toprule
				Alternative & Calm & Stormy \\
				\midrule
				Open-sea tours  & 46 & 9 \\
				Harbor tours & 31 & 23 \\
				\bottomrule
			\end{tabular}
		\end{center}
		%
		%
		\subsection*{Step 1: Identify}
		\[
		\begin{array}{llll}
			A_1 &= \textrm{Open-sea tours} &
			A_2 &= \textrm{Harbor tours} \\[3pt]
			S_1 &= \textrm{Calm} &
			S_2 &= \textrm{Stormy}
		\end{array}
		\]
		
		\subsection*{Step 2: Decide}
		
		\textbf{Maximax}
		
		\[
		\begin{array}{ll}
			M_1 &= \max\{46,9\}=46 \\
			M_2 &= \max\{31,23\}=31 \\
			A^*_{\mathrm{Maximax}} &= A_1 = \textrm{Open-sea tours}
		\end{array}
		\]
		
		\textbf{Maximin}
		
		\[
		\begin{array}{ll}
			m_1 &= \min\{46,9\}=9 \\
			m_2 &= \min\{31,23\}=23 \\
			A^*_{\mathrm{Maximin}} &= A_2 = \textrm{Harbor tours}
		\end{array}
		\]
		
		\subsection*{Step 3: Complete the table}
\begingroup\small
		
		\[
		\begin{array}{c|c|c}
			\text{Alternative} & \text{Max} & \text{Min}\\
			\cline{2-3}
			A_1 & 46 & 9\\
			A_2 & 31 & 23\\
			\hline
			A^*_{\mathrm{Maximax}} & A_1 & \\
			A^*_{\mathrm{Maximin}} & & A_2
		\end{array}
		\]
		
		\[
		\begin{array}{c|cc}
			& \multicolumn{2}{c}{\text{Alternative}}\\
			& A_1 & A_2\\
			\hline
			\text{Max} & 46 & 31\\
			\hline
			\text{Min} & 9 & 23\\
			\hline
			A^*_{\mathrm{Maximax}} & A_1 & \\
			A^*_{\mathrm{Maximin}} & & A_2
		\end{array}
		\]
