	\section*{Payoff Extraction Ladder}
	\vspace{-3mm}
	
	For each context, identify the decision alternatives, the states of nature, the uncertain event, and the payoff components. Then construct the corresponding payoff table. \vspace{-5mm}
	
	\section*{Context Descriptions}
	\vspace{-3mm}
	
	\begin{enumerate}
		
		\item
		A school event company must choose between a compact snack stand and a full snack stand before attendance is known. Attendance may be high or low. Analysts have already converted each possible consequence into direct profit measured in thousand USD. The compact stand produces a profit of 18 thousand USD under high attendance and 11 thousand USD under low attendance. The full stand produces a profit of 27 thousand USD under high attendance and 6 thousand USD under low attendance.
		
		\item
		An esports organizer must choose between a standard streaming setup and a professional streaming setup before online viewership is known. Viewership may be high or low. After all setup costs, the sponsorship agreement gives a fixed profit of 12 thousand EUR if the standard setup is chosen and 18 thousand EUR if the professional setup is chosen. The agreement does not change with the realized viewership level.
		
		\item
		A weekend pop-up business must choose between an indoor booth and a mobile stall before market demand is known. Demand may be strong or weak. The indoor booth contributes a fixed 550 thousand JPY to profit, while the mobile stall contributes a fixed 420 thousand JPY. Strong demand adds a fixed market bonus of 180 thousand JPY to either alternative. Weak demand instead reduces profit by a fixed 90 thousand JPY because of permit and cleanup expenses.
		
		\item
		A meal-preparation business must choose between a standard production plan and a premium production plan before demand is known. Demand may be strong or weak. The standard plan earns 2 thousand GBP per completed production batch and is expected to complete 12 batches under strong demand and 8 batches under weak demand. The premium plan earns 3 thousand GBP per completed production batch and is expected to complete 9 batches under strong demand and 5 batches under weak demand. There are no additional fixed adjustments.
		
		\item
		A local delivery firm must choose between a bicycle fleet and an electric-van fleet before traffic conditions are known. Traffic may be clear or congested. The bicycle fleet earns 4 thousand CNY per completed delivery batch and is expected to complete 14 delivery batches in clear traffic and 10 in congested traffic. The electric-van fleet earns 6 thousand CNY per completed delivery batch and is expected to complete 12 delivery batches in clear traffic and 8 in congested traffic. Clear traffic also adds a fixed scheduling bonus of 5 thousand CNY to either fleet, while congestion creates a fixed loss of 4 thousand CNY.
		
		\item
		A recycling contractor must choose between manual sorting and automated sorting before recovered-material prices are known. Prices may be high or low. Manual sorting contributes 3 million COP per processed recycling batch and automated sorting contributes 5 million COP per processed recycling batch. High material prices add another 4 million COP per processed batch to either alternative, while low material prices add only 1 million COP per processed batch. Manual sorting is expected to process 11 recycling batches when prices are high and 8 when they are low. Automated sorting is expected to process 9 recycling batches when prices are high and 7 when they are low.
		
		\item
		A city-tour operator must choose between a standard tour and a premium tour before tourism demand is known. Demand may be high or low. The standard tour contributes 2 thousand EUR per group booking and also provides a fixed contract contribution of 6 thousand EUR. The premium tour contributes 4 thousand EUR per group booking and provides a fixed contract contribution of 10 thousand EUR. High tourism demand adds another 3 thousand EUR per group booking to either tour, while low demand adds 1 thousand EUR per group booking. The standard tour is expected to complete 16 group bookings under high demand and 10 under low demand; the premium tour is expected to complete 12 group bookings under high demand and 7 under low demand.
		
		\item
		A food-distribution hub must choose between electric sorting and manual sorting before demand is known. Demand may be strong or soft. Electric sorting contributes 50 thousand JPY per processed distribution batch and provides a fixed contract contribution of 80 thousand JPY. Manual sorting contributes 30 thousand JPY per processed distribution batch and provides a fixed contract contribution of 120 thousand JPY. Strong demand adds another 20 thousand JPY per processed batch and a fixed distributor bonus of 60 thousand JPY. Soft demand adds 10 thousand JPY per processed batch but creates a fixed unused-capacity loss of 40 thousand JPY. Electric sorting is expected to process 14 distribution batches under strong demand and 9 under soft demand; manual sorting is expected to process 16 distribution batches under strong demand and 11 under soft demand.
		
		\item
		A solar-installation company must choose between a standard installation crew and an expanded installation crew before customer orders are known. Orders may be strong, regular, or weak. The standard crew contributes 3 thousand GBP per completed installation and provides a fixed contract contribution of 8 thousand GBP. The expanded crew contributes 4 thousand GBP per completed installation and provides a fixed contract contribution of 5 thousand GBP. Strong orders add 2 thousand GBP per completed installation and a fixed scheduling bonus of 6 thousand GBP. Regular orders add 1 thousand GBP per completed installation and no fixed adjustment. Weak orders reduce the contribution by 1 thousand GBP per completed installation and create a fixed idle-time loss of 4 thousand GBP. The standard crew is expected to complete 10 installations under strong orders, 7 under regular orders, and 4 under weak orders. The expanded crew is expected to complete 14, 9, and 5 installations under the same respective states.
		
		\item
		A consumer-electronics distributor must choose among a lean launch, a balanced launch, and an expanded launch before product demand is known. Demand may be strong, expected, or soft. The lean launch contributes 5 thousand CNY per shipment batch and provides a fixed supplier contribution of 18 thousand CNY. The balanced launch contributes 6 thousand CNY per shipment batch and provides a fixed supplier contribution of 12 thousand CNY. The expanded launch contributes 7 thousand CNY per shipment batch and provides a fixed supplier contribution of 5 thousand CNY. Strong demand adds 2 thousand CNY per shipment batch and a fixed launch bonus of 10 thousand CNY. Expected demand makes no additional per-batch or fixed adjustment. Soft demand reduces the contribution by 1 thousand CNY per shipment batch and creates a fixed markdown loss of 8 thousand CNY. The lean launch is expected to handle 12 shipment batches under strong demand, 9 under expected demand, and 6 under soft demand. The balanced launch is expected to handle 16, 11, and 7 shipment batches, while the expanded launch is expected to handle 20, 13, and 8 shipment batches under the same respective states.
		
		\item
		A coffee exporter must choose among contracted storage, a rented warehouse, and an automated distribution hub before export demand and shipping conditions are known. Conditions may be favorable, normal, or adverse. Contracted storage contributes 4 million COP per export lot handled but requires a fixed storage commitment of 12 million COP. The rented warehouse contributes 5 million COP per export lot but requires a fixed commitment of 20 million COP. The automated hub contributes 6 million COP per export lot but requires a fixed commitment of 32 million COP. Favorable conditions add 2 million COP per export lot and a fixed logistics bonus of 8 million COP. Normal conditions make no additional per-lot or fixed adjustment. Adverse conditions reduce the contribution by 2 million COP per export lot and create a fixed disruption loss of 10 million COP. Contracted storage is expected to handle 18 export lots under favorable conditions, 13 under normal conditions, and 8 under adverse conditions. The rented warehouse is expected to handle 22, 15, and 9 export lots, while the automated hub is expected to handle 26, 17, and 10 export lots under the same respective states.
	\end{enumerate}
