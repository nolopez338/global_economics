\author{}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

Create and deploy an \textbf{individual interactive decision webpage} based on a realistic economic or financial scenario. Use the \textbf{C10 reference dashboard} as the functional model and independently apply your learning from C1--C9.

\section*{Requirements}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.},leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Scenario and interactive model.} Present the decision context and editable payoff data. Include results for \textbf{Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes}, staged filtering, and a corresponding flow diagram.

\item \textbf{C10 evaluation.} Independently select and justify the most appropriate criterion for your scenario, considering the available information, objectives, risk attitude, and constraints. Interpret its recommendation, distinguish it from the staged-filtering outcome, and discuss limitations and sensitivity to changes in assumptions. Include this analysis on the webpage. Provide at least \textbf{two preloaded examples} that users can select to visualize and verify the correct functioning of the webpage's decision criteria, staged filtering, and flow diagram. Include a visible, accessible \textbf{hyperlink to the AI conversation used to create the webpage} on the same webpage.

\item \textbf{References and AI conversations.} Cite the sources used for your scenario and financial data, and identify your assumptions. Include a visible AI development record with accessible links to the AI conversations used.

\item \textbf{Submission.} Submit the \textbf{deployed webpage URL} and the \textbf{GitHub repository URL}, both accessible to the teacher.
\end{enumerate}

Ensure the webpage is accurate, functional, and easy to use.
