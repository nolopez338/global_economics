		\textbf{C8}: Compares the Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes decision criteria by contrasting their information requirements, risk orientations, recommended alternatives, and limitations.\par

		\textbf{C10}: Evaluates economic and financial decision-making scenarios by selecting and justifying the most appropriate decision criterion according to the information available, risk attitude, and decision context.}

		\SubsectionBox{1. Camera Rental Plans \score{C8-\sml{3}}}
A camera rental studio selects its inventory under uncertain demand. Profits are in thousand USD.

\vspace{-3mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
		Alternative & High & Normal & Low\\
		$P(S_j)$ & 0.2 & 0.6 & 0.2\\ \midrule
		Essential ($A_1$) & 100 & 90 & 70\\
		Shared ($A_2$) & 140 & 150 & 40\\
		Extensive ($A_3$) & 240 & 48 & -40\\
		Modular ($A_4$) & 170 & 130 & -20\\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{center} \vspace*{-3mm}

\columnbreak
Decision statistics (Regret = maximum regret):

\vspace{-3mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}\toprule
		Alt. & Max & Min & Regret & EMV\\ \midrule
		$A_1$ & 100 & 70 & 140 & 88\\
		$A_2$ & 150 & 40 & 100 & 126\\
		$A_3$ & 240 & -40 & 110 & 68.8\\
		$A_4$ & 170 & -20 & 90 & 108\\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{-3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\alph*)},leftmargin=*]
	\item Using the original camera rental payoffs, identify the alternative recommended by Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes, and report each criterion's deciding statistic value. \score{1}

	\item A subsidy $s$ (integer, $0\leq s\leq100$) increases the low-demand payoffs of $A_3$ and $A_4$ by $s$. An alternative is eligible only if all its payoffs are nonnegative. Determine each camera rental plan's eligibility based on the value of $s$. \score{0.5}

	\item Among eligible camera rental plans, find the smallest allowed subsidy $s$ at which the Maximin recommendation changes from $A_1$ to $A_4$ alone. Identify any tie at the threshold and distinguish it from a strict change. Respect the integer domain $0\leq s\leq100$; if no smallest value exists, state the threshold and the condition for a change. \score{1.5}
\end{enumerate}

Decision statistics (Regret = maximum regret):

\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}

		\bottomrule

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\alph*)},leftmargin=*]
	\item Using the original refreshment kiosk payoffs, identify the alternative recommended by Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes, and report each criterion's deciding statistic value. \score{1}

	\item A sponsor replaces $C_1$'s large-audience payoff of 96 with $g$ (integer, $96\leq g\leq160$). An alternative is eligible only if all its payoffs are at least 40. Determine each refreshment kiosk's eligibility based on the value of $g$. \score{0.5}

	\item Among eligible refreshment kiosks, find the smallest allowed sponsored payoff $g$ at which the Minimax Regret recommendation changes from $C_4$ to $C_1$ alone. Respect the integer domain $96\leq g\leq160$; if no smallest value exists, state the threshold and the condition for a change. \score{1.5}
\end{enumerate}
