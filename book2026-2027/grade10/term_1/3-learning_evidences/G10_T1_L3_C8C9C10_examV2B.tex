		\SubsectionBox{1. Camera Rental Plans \score{C8-\sml{3}}}
A camera rental studio selects its inventory under uncertain demand. Profits are in thousand USD.

\vspace{-3mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
		Alternative & High & Normal & Low\\
		$P(S_j)$ & 0.25 & 0.5 & 0.25\\ \midrule
				Essential ($A_1$) & 105 & 92 & 68\\
		Shared ($A_2$) & 145 & 155 & 38\\
		Extensive ($A_3$) & 245 & 50 & -45\\
		Modular ($A_4$) & 175 & 132 & -25\\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{center} \vspace*{-3mm}

Decision statistics (Regret = maximum regret):

\vspace{-4mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}\toprule
		Alt. & Max & Min & Regret & EMV\\ \midrule
				$A_1$ & 105 & 68 & 140 & 89.25\\
		$A_2$ & 155 & 38 & 100 & 123.25\\
		$A_3$ & 245 & -45 & 113 & 75\\
		$A_4$ & 175 & -25 & 93 & 103.5\\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{-4mm}

\noindent Using the original camera rental payoffs, identify the alternative recommended by Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes, and report each criterion's deciding statistic value. \sscore{1} A subsidy $s$ (integer, $0\leq s\leq105$) increases the low-demand payoffs of $A_3$ and $A_4$ by $s$. An alternative is eligible only if all its payoffs are nonnegative. Determine each camera rental plan's eligibility based on the value of $s$. \sscore{0.5} Among eligible camera rental plans, find the smallest allowed subsidy $s$ at which the Maximin recommendation changes from $A_1$ to $A_4$ alone. Identify any tie at the threshold and distinguish it from a strict change. Respect the integer domain $0\leq s\leq105$; if no smallest value exists, state the threshold and the condition for a change. \sscore{1.5}

Respect the integer domain $98\leq g\leq164$; if no smallest value exists, state the threshold and the condition for a change. \sscore{1.5}
