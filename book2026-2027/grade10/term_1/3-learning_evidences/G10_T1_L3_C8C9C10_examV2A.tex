		\SubsectionBox{1. Camera Rental Plans \score{C8-\sml{3}}}
A camera rental studio selects its inventory under uncertain demand. Profits are in thousand USD.

\vspace{-3mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
		Alternative & High & Normal & Low\\
		$P(S_j)$ & 0.3 & 0.5 & 0.2\\ \midrule
				Essential ($A_1$) & 110 & 95 & 65\\
		Shared ($A_2$) & 150 & 160 & 35\\
		Extensive ($A_3$) & 250 & 52 & -35\\
		Modular ($A_4$) & 180 & 135 & -15\\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{center} \vspace*{-3mm}

\columnbreak
Decision statistics (Regret = maximum regret):

\vspace{-3mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}\toprule
		Alt. & Max & Min & Regret & EMV\\ \midrule
				$A_1$ & 110 & 65 & 140 & 93.5\\
		$A_2$ & 160 & 35 & 100 & 132\\
		$A_3$ & 250 & -35 & 108 & 94\\
		$A_4$ & 180 & -15 & 80 & 118.5\\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{-3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\alph*)},leftmargin=*]
	\item Using the original camera rental payoffs, identify the alternative recommended by Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes, and report each criterion's deciding statistic value. \score{1}

	\item A subsidy $s$ (integer, $0\leq s\leq95$) increases the low-demand payoffs of $A_3$ and $A_4$ by $s$. An alternative is eligible only if all its payoffs are nonnegative. Determine each camera rental plan's eligibility based on the value of $s$. \score{0.5}

	\item Among eligible camera rental plans, find the smallest allowed subsidy $s$ at which the Maximin recommendation changes from $A_1$ to $A_4$ alone. Identify any tie at the threshold and distinguish it from a strict change. Respect the integer domain $0\leq s\leq95$; if no smallest value exists, state the threshold and the condition for a change. \score{1.5}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\alph*)},leftmargin=*]

	\item A sponsor replaces $C_1$'s large-audience payoff of 102 with $g$ (integer, $102\leq g\leq170$). An alternative is eligible only if all its payoffs are at least 44. Determine each refreshment kiosk's eligibility based on the value of $g$. \score{0.5}

\end{enumerate}
