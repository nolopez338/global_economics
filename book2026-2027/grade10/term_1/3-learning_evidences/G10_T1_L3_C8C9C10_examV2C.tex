		\SubsectionBox{1. Camera Rental Plans \score{C8-\sml{3}}}
A camera rental studio selects its inventory under uncertain demand. Profits are in thousand USD.

\vspace{-3mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
		Alternative & High & Normal & Low\\
		$P(S_j)$ & 0.2 & 0.5 & 0.3\\ \midrule
				Essential ($A_1$) & 115 & 100 & 75\\
		Shared ($A_2$) & 155 & 165 & 45\\
		Extensive ($A_3$) & 265 & 55 & -30\\
		Modular ($A_4$) & 190 & 145 & -10\\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{center} \vspace*{-3mm}

Decision statistics (Regret = maximum regret):

\vspace{-4mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}\toprule
		Alt. & Max & Min & Regret & EMV\\ \midrule
				$A_1$ & 115 & 75 & 150 & 95.5\\
		$A_2$ & 165 & 45 & 110 & 127\\
		$A_3$ & 265 & -30 & 110 & 71.5\\
		$A_4$ & 190 & -10 & 85 & 107.5\\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{-4mm}

\noindent Using the original camera rental payoffs, identify the alternative recommended by Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes, and report each criterion's deciding statistic value. \sscore{1} A subsidy $s$ (integer, $0\leq s\leq100$) increases the low-demand payoffs of $A_3$ and $A_4$ by $s$. An alternative is eligible only if all its payoffs are nonnegative. Determine each camera rental plan's eligibility based on the value of $s$. \sscore{0.5} Among eligible camera rental plans, find the smallest allowed subsidy $s$ at which the Maximin recommendation changes from $A_1$ to $A_4$ alone. Identify any tie at the threshold and distinguish it from a strict change. Respect the integer domain $0\leq s\leq100$; if no smallest value exists, state the threshold and the condition for a change. \sscore{1.5}

\noindent Using the original refreshment kiosk payoffs, identify the alternative recommended by Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes, and report each criterion's deciding statistic value. \sscore{1} A sponsor replaces $C_1$'s large-audience payoff of 108 with $g$ (integer, $108\leq g\leq180$). Determine each refreshment kiosk's eligibility based on the value of $g$. \sscore{0.5} Among eligible refreshment kiosks, find the smallest allowed sponsored payoff $g$ at which the Minimax Regret recommendation changes from $C_4$ to $C_1$ alone. Recalculate regret benchmarks using only eligible kiosks. Identify any tie at the threshold and distinguish it from a strict change. Respect the integer domain $108\leq g\leq180$; if no smallest value exists, state the threshold and the condition for a change. \sscore{1.5}
