\ExamHeader{10}{3}{DECISION-MAKING UNDER UNCERTAINTY}{1st}
\vspace{1mm}
\LearningObjective{Compare decision criteria and their limitations, and construct and interpret decision trees for staged filtering under uncertainty.}{
C8: Compares the Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes decision criteria by contrasting their information requirements, risk orientations, recommended alternatives, and limitations.\par
C9: Constructs and interprets decision trees for staged filtering rules, applying decision criteria in the specified order to identify the final alternative.}

\SubsectionBox{1. Repair Workshop Plans \score{C8-\sml{3}}}
A bicycle repair workshop chooses next quarter's capacity before demand is known. Profits are in thousand USD; forecast probabilities are shown below. \vspace{-3mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
		Alternative & High & Normal & Low\\
		$P(S_j)$ & 0.2 & 0.6 & 0.2\\ \midrule
		Large ($A_1$) & 120 & 25 & -20\\
		Flexible ($A_2$) & 85 & 65 & 10\\
		Basic ($A_3$) & 50 & 45 & 35\\
		Partner ($A_4$) & 70 & 75 & 20\\
		\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}

Decision statistics (Regret means maximum regret):\vspace{-3mm}

\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}\toprule
		Alternative & Max & Min & Regret & EMV\\ \midrule
		$A_1$ & 120 & -20 & 55 & 35\\
		$A_2$ & 85 & 10 & 35 & 58\\
		$A_3$ & 50 & 35 & 70 & 44\\
		$A_4$ & 75 & 20 & 50 & 63\\
		\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
	\vspace{-3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\alph*)},leftmargin=*]
	\item Report each criterion's recommended alternative(s), deciding statistic, and value. \score{1}
	
	\item A subsidy $s$ (integer, $0\leq s\leq30$, thousand USD) increases the low-demand payoffs of both $A_1$ and $A_2$ by $s$. All other payoffs and probabilities stay fixed. An alternative is eligible only if its profit is nonnegative in every state.
	Determine the ranges of $s$ for which each alternative is eligible. Find the smallest $s$ that changes the Maximin recommendation from part (a) to $A_2$ alone. At that $s$, report all four recommendations and deciding values among eligible alternatives. \score{2}
\end{enumerate}

\SubsectionBox{4. Refill Shop Expansion \score{C9-\sml{2}}}
A refill shop chooses next year's expansion plan $F_1$--$F_6$. Net profits are in thousand USD under strong, steady, and weak demand, with probabilities 0.3, 0.5, and 0.2.
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}\toprule
Alternative & Max & Min & Regret & EMV\\ \midrule
$F_1$ & 120 & 0 & 45 & 61\\
$F_2$ & 105 & 15 & 30 & 64.5\\
$F_3$ & 95 & 25 & 25 & 68.5\\
$F_4$ & 85 & 35 & 35 & 70\\
$F_5$ & 75 & 45 & 45 & 64\\
$F_6$ & 65 & 40 & 55 & 55\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
Apply these stages in order:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Minimax Regret: keep the 5 lowest Regret values.
\item EMV/Bayes: keep the 4 highest EMVs among survivors.
\item Maximax: keep the 2 highest Max values among survivors.
\item Maximin: select the highest Min among survivors.
\end{enumerate}
Construct the filtering tree and supporting table; include rules, values, eliminations, final choice, and a one-sentence interpretation. Use the shared rules for Questions 3--5.
