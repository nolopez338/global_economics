	\LearningObjective{Compare decision criteria and their limitations, and construct and interpret decision trees for staged filtering under uncertainty.}{
		\textbf{C8}: Compares the Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes decision criteria by contrasting their information requirements, risk orientations, recommended alternatives, and limitations.\par
		\textbf{C9}: Constructs and interprets decision trees for staged filtering rules, applying decision criteria in the specified order to identify the final alternative. \par
		\textbf{C10}: Evaluates economic and financial decision-making scenarios by selecting and justifying the most appropriate decision criterion according to the information available, risk attitude, and decision context.}

		\SubsectionBox{1. Camera Rental Plans \score{C8-\sml{3}}}
A camera rental studio selects its inventory under uncertain demand. Profits are in thousand USD.

\vspace{-3mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
		Alternative & High & Normal & Low\\
		$P(S_j)$ & 0.15 & 0.6 & 0.25\\ \midrule
				Essential ($A_1$) & 120 & 105 & 78\\
		Shared ($A_2$) & 165 & 175 & 48\\
		Extensive ($A_3$) & 280 & 58 & -42\\
		Modular ($A_4$) & 200 & 155 & -22\\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{center} \vspace*{-3mm}

Decision statistics (Regret = maximum regret):

\vspace{-4mm}
\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
	\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}\toprule
		Alt. & Max & Min & Regret & EMV\\ \midrule
				$A_1$ & 120 & 78 & 160 & 100.5\\
		$A_2$ & 175 & 48 & 115 & 141.75\\
		$A_3$ & 280 & -42 & 120 & 66.3\\
		$A_4$ & 200 & -22 & 100 & 117.5\\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{-4mm}

\noindent Using the original camera rental payoffs, identify the alternative recommended by Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, and EMV/Bayes, and report each criterion's deciding statistic value. \sscore{1} A subsidy $s$ (integer, $0\leq s\leq110$) increases the low-demand payoffs of $A_3$ and $A_4$ by $s$. An alternative is eligible only if all its payoffs are nonnegative. Determine each camera rental plan's eligibility based on the value of $s$. \sscore{0.5} Among eligible camera rental plans, find the smallest allowed subsidy $s$ at which the Maximin recommendation changes from $A_1$ to $A_4$ alone. Identify any tie at the threshold and distinguish it from a strict change. Respect the integer domain $0\leq s\leq110$; if no smallest value exists, state the threshold and the condition for a change. \sscore{1.5}
