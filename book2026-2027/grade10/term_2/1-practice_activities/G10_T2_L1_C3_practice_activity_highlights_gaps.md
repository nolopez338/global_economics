Existing support reused without additions:

- [Canonical loader](../../../preamble/highlights/specific_highlights.tex) and [index](../../../preamble/highlights/specific_highlights_index.md): imports now use `highlights/highlights.tex` and `highlights/specific_highlights.tex`.
- [Compatibility adapter](../../../preamble/highlights/highlights_compat_ttwo.tex), `practicehighlightlegend@G10-T2-C3-coordinated`: the existing three-column opening legend is suitable and is reused before Introduction. Its grouped notation remains appropriate for the concise legend.
- [Table infrastructure](../../../preamble/highlights/highlights_tables.tex), `\HighlightDetailedTable` and `\COneToneDetailedHeaders`: already support the required four columns and continuation headings. The practice passes explicit atomic rows, without `\SpecificHighlightUnion`, `\HighlightRenderSelected`, or concept-merging union rows. No new rendering capability is needed for this local table. The existing union renderer groups by concept and would not preserve these atomic distinctions.
- [Field lookup interface](../../../preamble/highlights/highlights_catalog.tex), `\HighlightEffectiveField`: reused for applicable C3 probability, expectation, and break-even definitions. Grouped notation fields are not copied into atomic notation cells.
- [Roles](../../../preamble/highlights/highlights_roles.tex): the existing event, quantity, probability, cumulative, and expectation wrappers suffice for local handling. No new color or role is required. Function notation remains contextual; no artificial transformation-probability role is introduced.
- `g10-t2-c3-coordinated-10-break` already supplies the individual break-even concept, notation, definition, and membership. Its definition is reused; the practice retains the fixed-payoff assumption, exact roots, zero interpretation, and sign direction for each introductory alternative. No break-even ecosystem gap is asserted.

Example-specific meanings are intentionally retained in the practice at `tab:notation-reference`, linked to `ex:intro-one` (clothing inventory) and `ex:intro-two` (coffee-shop disruption). The empty `examples` fields of the C3 contexts are not filled or treated as a requirement to move these meanings into shared records. No examples were invented. Questions, worked solutions, navigation labels, and the existing highlight toggle are outside the edits; Olympiad material is excluded.

No ecosystem additions were implemented. No compilation, rendering, tests, source review, or validation was performed after editing.
