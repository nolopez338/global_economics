\section{Warm-up: delivery costs}
\begin{frame}[t]{Warm-up: a delivery-cost limit}
A school club orders supplies. Delivery costs depend on whether
a second trip is needed. Use these chances for the next order:
\par\smallskip
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}Xll@{}}
\toprule
Delivery outcome & Total delivery cost & Chance\\\midrule
\A{One trip} & \M{USD 12} & \Prob{7 out of 10}\\[5pt]
\A{Second trip needed} & \M{USD 28} & \Prob{3 out of 10}\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\par\smallskip
\Ask{What is the chance the delivery cost stays at or below the club's
USD 12 limit? Explain.}
{\color{GETeal}\footnotesize Think and discuss: 60 seconds.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Intuitive check and connection}
The chance is \CDF{7 out of 10 (70\%)}: the \M{USD 12} cost meets the limit;
the \M{USD 28} cost exceeds it.
\par\smallskip
\textbf{``At or below'' includes exactly equal to the cutoff}, so the
\M{USD 12} outcome counts.
\par\medskip
\textbf{Into the lesson}\par
A \CDF{cumulative distribution function (CDF)} orders the monetary outcomes
and organizes the \CDF{chance accumulated up to any cutoff}.
It uses the same outcome probabilities, so one representation can answer
whether a limit is respected as the cutoff changes.
\end{frame}
